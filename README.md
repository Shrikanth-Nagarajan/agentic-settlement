# Settlement skills for Kai

A concept prototype for an agentic settlement layer on a multi-currency payments platform, built by Shri Nagarajan.

- Prototype: https://shrikanth-nagarajan.github.io/agentic-settlement/
- One-page brief: https://shrikanth-nagarajan.github.io/agentic-settlement/brief.html

## What to try

1. **Business view, Batches.** Open Wednesday's GBP batch. Kai can't explain £14.20 of it, so it says so and opens a case.
2. **Settlement ops.** Open E6, approve the fix, then go back to the business view. The batch now adds up and Kai has replied.
3. **"Card scheme funding is late".** Approve or hold the pre-funding proposal in ops, and watch the merchant's notice change.
4. **"Refunds exceed sales".** The merchant gets three ways to cover a negative batch, with a safe default.
5. **Settlement setup.** Settle-to-spend: stop converting a currency the business also pays out in.

Switch between the UK and EU entities with the Market control.

## Notes

Independent concept, not affiliated with or endorsed by Airwallex. All businesses, people and figures are fictional. Fees, FX rates and margins are illustrative. Kai's answers are scripted for the demo.

Single HTML file, no build step, no tracking.
